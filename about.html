Meet Shelbi.

Shelbi Bale is an award-winning graphic designer with a background in Visual Studies and User Experience. Over her course as an undergrad student, she has worked as a creative in many different fast-paced, professional environments including news, sports, and branding. Her skills include typography, layout, digital and illustration, with career interests in graphic design, UX/UI design, and digital media production.

As a dedicated creative and a self-proclaimed art history nerd, she loves finding new ways to create engaging designs in both her professional and personal works.
